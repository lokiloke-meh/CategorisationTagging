# Termination of BounceBit (BB) network support and migration to BNB Chain (BEP-20)

Sep 3, 2026

Sep 3, 2026

![Termination of BounceBit (BB) network support and migration to BNB Chain (BEP-20)](https://static.bybit.com/download/noce/announcement_img/dea48da554ef6ea95b90e5f16f5a284f.png?auto=webp&quality=80&width=640)

Bybit is committed to providing a secure and reliable trading experience. As part of our ongoing review of network stability and operational security, Bybit will terminate support for the BounceBit network. At the same time, BB tokens will be migrated from the BounceBit network to BNB Chain at a **1:1 ratio**.

For more detailed information about this transition, please refer to the [official project team announcement](https://x.com/bouncebit/status/2090785561800061430).

## Key timelines and notice

* **BounceBit Network:** Deposits and withdrawals for BB via the native BounceBit network have been suspended.
* **BNB Chain (BEP-20):** Deposits and withdrawals for BB via BNB Chain are expected to reopen on **Sep 4, 2026, 8AM UTC**. Please note that this timeline is subject to operational progress and coordination with the project team.

### New smart contract address on BNB Chain

* **BB (BEP-20):** `0xe0620aBeA429A66Ba69d9a0cdE9aB93dc15bB9c4`

Rest assured that other activities, including token trading, will not be affected by this network migration.

Thank you for your continued support and understanding.
