## MarketWideBoardLotSizeFrameworkChanges (1 items)
  * **Title**: Consultation on Board Lot Framework Enhancements in the Hong Kong Securities Market
  * **Summary**: HKEX is consulting on enhancing the Board Lot Framework for Equities 1 and REITs to boost market competitiveness. Key proposals include lowering the board lot value floor from HK$2,000 to HK$1,000, introducing a ceiling of HK$50,000, and standardizing units to eight options (1, 50, 100, 500, 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, 10,000 shares). Phase One applies the floor/ceiling guidance to existing issuers while new issuers adopt all components. Phase Two mandates standardized units for existing issuers post-transition to uncertificated shareholding. The consultation closes on March 12, 2026.
  * **Source URL**: https://www.hkex.com.hk/-/media/HKEX-Market/Services/Circulars-and-Notices/Participant-and-Members-Circulars/SEHK/2025/EPD_CE_001_25_e.pdf

## MockDRDisasterRecoverySession (2 items)
  * **Title**: HKEX Backbone Network Upgrade for Securities Market Systems - Market Rehearsals Update
  * **Summary**: The second mandatory Market Rehearsal (MR2) for the HKEX backbone network upgrade is scheduled for Saturday, May 30, 2026, from 09:00 to 13:15. MR2 will involve upgrading the entire backbone network at the HKEX primary site while the backup site remains unchanged. All Exchange Participants (EPs) and China Connect Exchange Participants (CCEPs) must participate by simulating normal daily operations, including submitting orders and verifying connectivity via SFTP where applicable. Further details regarding subsequent rehearsals and post-release tests will be announced later.

  * **Title**: Attachment 1: Activity Rundowns for Upgrade of HKEX Backbone Network for Securities Market Systems Market Rehearsal 2 (MR2) on 30 May 2026
  * **Summary**: Market Rehearsal 2 (MR2) for the HKEX Backbone Network Upgrade is scheduled for Saturday, May 30, 2026. Key activities include OTP-CSC readiness checks on Friday, May 29, 2026, after market close (network configuration, component power-on, production database backups). On MR2, trading simulations will occur during the Opening Call Auction (9:15am–9:25am) and Morning/Afternoon Continuous Auctions. Northbound trading has specific order rules regarding price limits (+/- 10% or +/- 20%), maximum sizes (e.g., 1M shares), and short selling restrictions. Pre-Trade Checking requires IIID input for institutional orders, and ECP SFTP testing is available from 9:00am–1:15pm, though submissions should generally be avoided unless operationally necessary. MR2 can be cancelled if Typhoon Signal No. 8 or higher is issued between 7:00am and before 9:00am on May 30th.
